About Dylan

Dylan has been practicing yoga for over 15 years, and one of the reasons she teaches is because she needs the practice too.

For her, yoga is about a lot more than flexibility or fancy poses. It is about learning how to breathe, pay attention, create space, move more comfortably, regulate your nervous system, and keep returning to yourself.

She is an experienced certified yoga teacher with a trauma-informed, beginner-welcoming approach. Her classes are supportive, real, and free of performative yoga energy.

If you wobble, laugh, take a break, or use every prop in the room, you are doing it right.
