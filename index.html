Our Philosophy

Yoga is not about the perfect pose. It’s about coming home to yourself, breath by breath.

Come as you are

No experience required. No perfect body. Just show up exactly as you are.

Move with intention

Every posture is an invitation to listen. Strength and softness live together here.

Reset & return

Leave the noise at the door. Find stillness that travels with you off the mat.

Be fully you

This is your space to feel, to rest, to grow — without judgment or comparison.

Meet Dylan

Dylan has been sharing yoga for over 12 years. Their teaching is rooted in accessibility, breath, and the radical idea that everyone belongs on the mat.

Trained in multiple lineages with a deep focus on trauma-informed practices and meeting students exactly where they are. Whether you’re brand new or have been practicing for decades, you’ll find a home here.

Classes emphasize kindness over achievement and presence over perfection.
